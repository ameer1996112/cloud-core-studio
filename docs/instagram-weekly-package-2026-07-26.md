## Post 1 — Reel: welcome to the studio

**Purpose:** Make the account immediately understandable and give new visitors a calm, trustworthy reason to choose the relevant path.

**Format:** 18–24 seconds, 1080 × 1920, authentic footage, natural colour; navy/ivory/gold lower-third only. Cover: `أهلاً في Cloud & Core` / `הבית שלך לתנועה, כוח ורוגע`.

**Hook (0–2s):** `أهلاً في Cloud & Core بحرفيش ☁️` / Hebrew subtitle: `ברוכות הבאות ל-Cloud & Core בחורפיש`

**Caption — Arabic first:**
