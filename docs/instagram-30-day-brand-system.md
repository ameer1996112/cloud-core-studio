# Cloud & Core Instagram — 30-Day Brand and Content System

Prepared: 2026-07-22  
Campaign window: 2026-07-26 through 2026-08-22  
Status: Draft for owner approval. Nothing in this document authorizes automatic publishing or ad changes.

## 1. Business outcomes

The first 30 days optimize for registrations, not follower count.

- 15 booked adult trial lessons.
- 12 booked children’s trial lessons.
- At least 50% of trial attendees convert to a paid plan.
- Every children’s lead is traceable to Instagram or Meta ads.
- Every adult registration is traceable to Instagram, the app, WhatsApp, or Meta ads.

## 2. The two customer journeys

### Adult women

Instagram content or ad → Cloud & Core app for immediate booking → ₪80 trial → suitable adult plan.

WhatsApp remains available for women who need help choosing a class.

Adult plans:

- Trial: ₪80.
- One class weekly: ₪280/month.
- Two classes weekly: ₪350/month.
- Ten-class ticket: ₪700, valid for 90 days.
- Packages can be used for aerial yoga, mat Pilates, and HOT Pilates.

Limited launch offer:

- First 10 qualifying new members.
- Member must join within 24 hours after the trial.
- First month is ₪140 for the one-class-weekly plan or ₪175 for the two-class-weekly plan.
- The ten-class ticket stays ₪700.

### Children and parents

Instagram content or ad → dedicated WhatsApp conversation with the studio → ₪80 trial → suitable recurring group.

Children’s program:

- Maximum six children per group.
- One class per week.
- Ten-month program.
- ₪280 monthly, or ₪2,500 upfront instead of ₪2,800.
- New groups open after enough qualified demand is collected.

Required WhatsApp opening message:

> مرحباً، وصلت من إنستغرام ومهتمة بحصة تجريبية لليوغا الهوائية للأطفال. عمر طفلي/طفلتي هو \_\_\_.

Hebrew fallback:

> היי, הגעתי מאינסטגרם ואני מתעניינת בשיעור ניסיון ביוגה אווירית לילדים. גיל הילד/ה הוא \_\_\_.

The first studio reply should ask for the child’s age, previous experience, preferred days, and preferred trial date. Target response time: within two business hours.

## 3. Brand system

### Positioning

Cloud & Core is a warm premium boutique studio in Hurfeish. The account should communicate confidence, strength, safety, calm, personal attention, and belonging. It should not feel like an aggressive gym or a discount-led activity account.

### Visual rules

- Navy `#0B1D3A`: primary text, strong contrast, and CTA bars.
- Ivory `#FAF7F2`: primary background.
- Gold `#D4AF6A`: restrained accent lines and icon details.
- Warm sand `#E8DFD1`: soft secondary surfaces.
- Slate `#6F7A8C`: supporting copy.
- Latin display type: Cormorant Garamond.
- Arabic: Noto Sans Arabic.
- Hebrew and Latin utility copy: Assistant.
- Reel and Story canvas: 1080 × 1920.
- Feed carousel canvas: 1080 × 1350.
- Covers use one short Arabic-first promise and one concise Hebrew support line.
- Use a subtle cloud-line motif and a small brand mark; do not place a large logo over every photo.
- Keep authentic class footage natural. Do not apply heavy color filters.

### Content labels

Use small consistent labels so a visitor can understand the grid quickly:

- للنساء · נשים
- للأطفال · ילדים
- Pilates
- ابدئي من هنا · מתחילים כאן
- الأسعار · מחירים
- سؤال وجواب · שאלות

### Highlight structure

1. Start Here
2. Adults
3. Kids
4. Classes
5. Prices
6. Schedule
7. Results
8. FAQ

### Three pinned posts

1. Welcome to Cloud & Core: the studio, location, instructor, and atmosphere.
2. Adult guide: class types, trial price, plans, and app booking.
3. Children’s guide: ages 7+, trial, group size, program price, and WhatsApp registration.

Existing posts remain published. Older content is reviewed only after the new system is established.

## 4. Language and editorial rules

- Arabic is the primary language.
- Reels spoken in Arabic receive concise Hebrew subtitles.
- Important conversion captions include a shorter Hebrew section after the Arabic section.
- Use simple conversational Arabic appropriate for the local audience.
- Use one clear action per post: book in the app, ask on WhatsApp, save, share, or comment.
- Never invent testimonials, scarcity, results, class footage, or customer experiences.
- Avoid claims that a class treats, cures, or guarantees relief from a medical condition.
- Identifiable children require explicit written parent permission before publication.
- Without permission, film hammocks, hands, feet, silhouettes, instructor demonstrations, or non-identifying angles.
- AI may support layouts and decorative elements but must not fabricate customers or studio classes.

## 5. Weekly publishing rhythm

- Sunday: Reel.
- Tuesday: educational or pricing carousel.
- Thursday: Reel.
- Stories: Sunday through Thursday, four or five days each week.
- Saturday evening: next week’s package is delivered to the owner for approval.
- The owner is the sole approver.
- No approval means no publication.

### Story rhythm

Rotate these five Story types each week:

1. Today at the studio: one authentic moment.
2. Availability: only show real open spaces.
3. Question sticker or poll: adult class choice or children’s age group.

Never publish children’s schedules, names, arrival times, or other information that creates a safety or privacy risk.

## 8. Weekly media handoff

Upload approximately ten vertical clips each week, ideally 5–12 seconds each:

- Two wide studio shots.
- Two instructor-to-camera clips.
- Three adult movement clips.
- Two children’s clips with verified permission or non-identifying framing.
- One detail or atmosphere clip.

Label every upload with audience, class type, date, and consent status. Example:

`kids-aerial-2026-07-24-consent-yes-01.mp4`

Do not send children’s full names in filenames.

## 9. Approval checklist

Before any publication, the owner confirms:

- Price and offer are still valid.
- Availability and scarcity statements are true.
- Every visible participant has publication permission.
- Arabic and Hebrew meaning are accurate.
- The app or WhatsApp action is correct.
- Date, location, and class details are correct.
- The final cover and caption are approved.

## 10. Measurement

Track weekly:

- Adult app visits and booked trials from Instagram.
- Adult WhatsApp questions from Instagram.
- Children’s WhatsApp leads.
- Children’s trials booked and attended.
- Paid-plan conversions after trials.
- Reach, views, shares, saves, comments, and profile visits.
- Cost per messaging conversation and cost per booked trial from ads.

Use these lead stages for children:

`new lead → replied → trial booked → attended → joined → declined/no response`

Follower growth is supporting context, not the primary outcome.

Current observations:

- The women’s campaign is producing deeper conversations efficiently: seven starts and seven first replies.
- The children’s campaign buys inexpensive attention but loses more people between link click and conversation.
- Both active ad sets target Hurfeish within a 10-mile radius at approximately $5/day.
- The children’s campaign currently opens Instagram Direct, while the agreed children’s sales process belongs on WhatsApp.
- No campaign, ad set, budget, targeting, or creative was changed during this audit.

Recommended next ad decision after owner review:

1. Keep both campaigns running while they collect enough data.
2. Add booked-trial and paid-plan outcomes to the weekly report; Meta conversations alone are not sufficient.
3. Prepare a WhatsApp-based children’s variant with the dedicated prefilled message.
4. Do not replace the current kids campaign until the WhatsApp variant and tracking path are verified.
5. Use the strongest approved organic adult and children’s post as the next creative test.
