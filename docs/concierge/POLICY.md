# Concierge policy

Studio time is `Asia/Jerusalem`; timestamps are UTC. Quiet hours are 20:30–08:00. Only urgent
class operations and genuinely expiring customer actions may bypass them. Member payment
failures normally wait.

Priority is: urgent class operations; expiring waitlist/actionable payment; first booking,
booking confirmation, and payment success; renewals/reminders; weekly schedule; recommendation
and retention; low-value prompts.

One non-urgent external interruption is allowed per six hours and two per rolling 24 hours.
Promotional communication is additionally capped at one local day and three rolling seven
days. Consent is recipient/channel/purpose/locale/version-specific. A missing approval or
consent is a suppression, never a provider failure or cross-channel opt-out bypass.

First bookings use in-app plus consented WhatsApp, falling back to push. Repeat bookings use
in-app plus push, with consented WhatsApp only when push is unavailable. A 24-hour reminder is
push only. Two-hour WhatsApp is conditional. Weekly schedule is push only. Class cancellation
may use push and WhatsApp in parallel.

Payment and subscription events normalize to one outcome. Routine success never uses WhatsApp;
automatic retries do not interrupt members; recovered payment cancels pending failure actions.
Retention WhatsApp is limited to once per inactivity episode.
